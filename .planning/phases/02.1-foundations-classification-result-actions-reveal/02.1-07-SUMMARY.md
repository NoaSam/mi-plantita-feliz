## Decisions Made
- **PendingClassificationListener inside BrowserRouter:** `useNavigate` requires the router context. Placing the listener at the component layer (vs inside `AuthContext`) keeps the auth provider framework-agnostic and lets HMR pair mount/unmount cleanly.
- **`unclassifiedCount` derived from `context === 'unclassified' || !context`:** Defensive against rows that pre-date Plan 01's migration (the DEFAULT applies retroactively but until refetch, in-memory rows may not carry the field). Treating undefined-context as unclassified is the same semantic.
- **History refetch on location change AND on CustomEvent:** Double trigger covers two paths — back button to /mis-plantas after detail view changes context, and the parallel auth flow that resolves classification while History wasn't even mounted (StrictMode + tab switching).
